# YT Auto Downloader (vd6s)

This Chrome extension automates the process of downloading multiple YouTube videos via the website `vd6s.net`.

## How to Install

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open Google Chrome.
3. In the address bar, type `chrome://extensions/` and press Enter.
4. In the top right corner, toggle the **Developer mode** switch to ON.
5. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top left.
6. Select the folder containing these extension files (e.g., the `extension` folder).
7. The extension is now installed and should appear in your Chrome toolbar (you may need to click the puzzle piece icon to pin it).

## How to Use

1. Click on the extension icon in your toolbar to open the popup.
2. **Add Links:** Paste a YouTube URL into the text box and click **Add**. The link will appear in the "Download Queue" below. Repeat this for all the videos you want to download.
   - If you make a mistake, you can click the **Remove** button next to any pending link.
   - If you close the popup, the extension will remember any links that haven't been processed yet.
3. **Start Downloading:** Once your queue is ready, click **Start Download**.
4. The extension will automatically open a new tab to `vd6s.net/en5/`.
5. **Do not close this new tab.** The extension will automatically paste the URL, select the highest available video quality, and initiate the download.
6. The extension waits for the current download to completely finish before moving to the next link in your queue.
7. You can click the extension icon at any time to check the status of your queue (Pending, Complete, or Failed).
8. Once all items are processed, the tab will close automatically.

## Notes
- Ensure your browser settings allow automatic downloads without prompting for a save location every time, or you will have to manually confirm each save.
- If a video fails to download, it will be marked as "FAILED" in the popup, and the extension will automatically proceed to the next link.
